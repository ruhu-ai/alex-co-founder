Hiring privacy and human-review notice

This is the general Co-Founder hiring notice. Each published role links to a role-specific notice naming the hiring company, privacy contact and retention policy.

How applications are handled

Applications use a role-scoped form. Alex may organize exact job-related evidence into a cited, criterion-by-criterion summary. Alex does not rank candidates, recommend a hire or rejection, or make an employment decision. An authorized human makes every advance, hold, evidence-request and decline decision.

Your choices

The role-specific notice explains how to request a human contact, accommodation, correction, withdrawal, access, export or deletion. Applicable law and a documented legal hold may affect deletion.

What is not shared

Internal assessments, other candidates, approvals, provider credentials and private founder notes are not exposed through public pages. Candidate identity is encrypted separately and applications are excluded from general founder memory and search.
