Hiring privacy and human-review notice

Preview notice — synthetic evaluation only. No production role may use this notice until the applicable employment, privacy, accessibility, jurisdiction and retention review is complete.

How applications are handled

Applications arrive by email. Automated systems may organize job-related evidence into a cited, criterion-by-criterion summary. They do not rank candidates, recommend a hire or rejection, or make an employment decision. An authorized human makes every advance, hold, evidence-request and decline decision.

Your choices

You may request a human contact, an accommodation, correction, withdrawal, access, export or deletion by replying to the role email or contacting the named hiring representative. Production contact details and retention periods will appear here only after qualified review.

What is not shared

Internal assessments, other candidates, approvals, provider credentials and private founder notes are not exposed through this page. This page accepts no candidate data and creates no candidate account.
