# Competition demo script — Hiring Operations

The current competition path demonstrates the production-shaped Hiring
workflow. It does not depend on a bundled application portal or synthetic
provider service.

## Before recording

- Sign in to the live app with the Founder account supplied in the competition
  submission.
- Confirm Alex Mail, Founder Calendar, and Alex Calendar are Ready.
- Confirm the H7 kill switch and exact release projection are in their intended
  competition state.
- Use a dedicated test role and fictional applicant data. Do not expose real
  credentials, CV content, email addresses, or applicant identity in the film.

## Four-minute path

| Beat | Surface | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Alex chat | Start `/hiring` with a natural Founder role description. |
| 2 | Hiring role workspace | Review the grounded role package and approve publication. |
| 3 | Public job page | Show the separated applicant form and submit fictional test data. |
| 4 | Candidate workspace | Alex prepares criterion-linked evidence; open citations in the restricted viewer. |
| 5 | Founder decision | Commit ADVANCE and activate the bounded `SCHEDULE_INTERVIEW` goal. |
| 6 | Activity / email | Show causally linked reply handling, availability recheck, and interview booking. |
| 7 | Audit / Cloud Run | Show ordered activity, idempotent receipts, and the serving Cloud Run revision. |

## Narration boundary

Alex prepares and coordinates; the Founder owns hiring decisions and the
approval of irreversible actions. Candidate material stays role-scoped and is
excluded from general search, profile memory, and company knowledge. Provider
OAuth scopes alone never authorize an effect.
